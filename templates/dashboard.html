How status and uptime are calculated

  • Healthy means the last check returned the expected status within the timeout and under the slow threshold. Degraded is one failed check, Down is two in a row.
  • Uptime = successful checks ÷ total checks × 100 for the window. Timeouts count as failures. Paused apps (maintenance) are not checked and not counted.
  • Only add systems you own or have permission to monitor.
